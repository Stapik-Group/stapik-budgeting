# Adding and changing entries

An entry has a name, a category, a type (expense or income), a planned amount and an actual amount. Both amounts are optional: leave the actual amount empty until the money has really moved.

* The two buttons with arrows move an entry up or down. The order matters, because the *Remaining* column is a running balance down the list.
* The pencil edits the entry, the cross deletes it.
* **Edit → Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`) takes back the last change, and the menu names it, for example "Undo Delete entry".

The month starts from its starting amount. *Remaining* is that amount, plus income, minus expenses, up to and including the line.
